.. _evs-thirdparty--original-credit-notices:

Original credit notices
-----------------------

The imported Demo contains the asset-local notices; the package sample retains the same notices under ``Samples~/Demo``:

-  ``Art/Credits.md``, including the individual model and animation files.
-  ``Art/Asphalt/Credits.md`` and ``Art/Desert Sand/Credits.md``, including texture preparation details.
-  ``Audio/Credits.md`` and ``Audio/Surfaces/Credits.md``, including source recordings and edits.

Reusable effects are supplied in the package at ``Resources/Effects/Jet``, ``Nitro``, ``Weapons`` and ``Water``.

Bundled reusable audio defaults also retain their notices in the package's ``Resources/Audio/Credits.md``. Keep the applicable asset-local notices with reused content. The original package code's license is recorded separately in ``LICENSE.md``.
