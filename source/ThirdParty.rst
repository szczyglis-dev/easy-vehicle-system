Third-party credits
===================

The supplied models, recordings and animations have their own attribution and license files. Consult those files when reusing the sample content.

+---------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------+
| Content                               | Credit location                                         |
+=======================================+=========================================================+
| Demo models, materials and animations | Sample ``Art/Credits.md`` and asset-local notices       |
+---------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------+
| Demo audio                            | Sample ``Audio/Credits.md`` and recording-local notices |
+---------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------+
| Tire-surface audio                    | Sample ``Audio/Surfaces/Credits.md``                    |
+---------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------+
| Bundled reusable audio defaults       | Package ``Runtime/Defaults/Audio/Credits.md``           |
+---------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------+

With the asset-package layout, the sample content is under Demo. With UPM, use the imported Demo folder. Preserve the applicable notices with assets you redistribute. The package's original code license is in LICENSE.md; it is separate from third-party content terms.
